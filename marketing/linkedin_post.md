**Today we're open-sourcing CAB-FF — the Flourishing & Faithfulness Benchmark for Christian AI alignment.**

In December, Gloo announced that today's frontier language models average just 48 out of 100 on the Faith dimension of their privately-held Christian benchmark. They reported the finding. They did not publish the methodology, the questions, the judge prompts, or the alignment indicators.

CAB-FF is the open replicate.

What's in it:

✅ 820+ questions across 8 dimensions (the 7 from Harvard's Human Flourishing Program that Gloo uses, plus an eighth — Vocation & Witness — that secular flourishing frameworks cannot reach by construction)

✅ 5 question types including adversarial drift probes, multi-turn pushback dialogues that test sycophancy, and Christian-vs-neutral comparative pairs that directly measure secular drift

✅ 9 named, tradition-aware judge personas (Reformed, Catholic, Orthodox, Wesleyan, Pentecostal, Anglican, Baptist, plus a pastoral counselor and an academic theologian)

✅ 40 published alignment indicators with explicit detection criteria — including 18 distinctively Christian probes targeting the failure modes Gloo named publicly

✅ Explicit Drift Index and Sycophancy Index as secondary metrics

✅ Plug-and-play provider adapters: Anthropic, OpenAI, LiteLLM (100+ models), mock-for-CI

✅ A 5-second smoke test with no API key required

Everything — code, prompts, judges, the dataset — is CC BY-SA 4.0.

If you build, evaluate, deploy, or invest in AI for Christian audiences, CAB-FF gives you a defensible, reproducible measurement framework.
