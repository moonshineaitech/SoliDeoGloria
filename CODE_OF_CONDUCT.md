# Code of Conduct

CAB-FF evaluates theology. Theology is contested. That makes
contributor conduct doubly important.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, ethnicity,
ability, gender identity or expression, national origin, denomination,
or theological position within historic Christianity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Engaging tradition-specific claims with charity and substance
- Disagreeing with another contributor's theology while respecting them
- Reading source material before pushing back on a question
- Citing Scripture, confessions, or primary sources when proposing edits
- Welcoming questions from people newer to Christian theology or AI
  alignment

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Personal attacks, ad hominem, slurs, or sustained dismissal
- Caricaturing or mocking a contributor's tradition
- Doxxing, harassment, or threats — in any form, public or private
- Treating disagreement as bad faith without evidence
- Polemics that aren't tied to a specific question, axis, or scorer

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — repository, issues, PR
review, discussions, and any official communication channels.

## Enforcement

Report issues to research@solideogloria.ai with the subject line
"CAB-FF Code of Conduct". Maintainers will:

1. Acknowledge receipt within 5 business days.
2. Review the report privately.
3. Take action proportional to the severity (warning, edit-only
   restriction, temporary or permanent ban).

We will not retaliate against good-faith reports.
